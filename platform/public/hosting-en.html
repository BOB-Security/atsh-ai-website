HOSTING & OPERATIONS

Hosting and operations

We select an operating model based on the actual technology and workload, rather than placing every site on the same plan.

Hosting patterns

  • WordPress/PHP: traditional or reseller hosting where resources and recovery fit.
  • Static websites: a suitable publishing platform when no backend is required.
  • Web apps with APIs and databases: an application host and managed database.
  • AI assistants: model access, secure application runtime, approved knowledge and usage limits.

What we document

Resource limits, domain and DNS, TLS, backup retention, update responsibilities and recurring cost.

Backups: recoverability is not promised until an actual restore test is recorded. Coverage depends on the agreement and may include files, database or both.
