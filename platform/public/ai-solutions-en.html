AI assistant solutions

Assistants can answer common questions, route requests and support customer service using approved knowledge and clear operating boundaries.

Service safeguards

  • The assistant must not invent prices, delivery dates or guarantees.
  • It should escalate when approved knowledge is insufficient or a person is requested.
  • Each customer’s knowledge and conversations must remain isolated.
  • Usage limits, cost and connected channels are agreed before activation.
  • Integration secrets stay server-side and are never sent to the browser.

Provider availability and integrations are not considered active until configured and tested.
